# R159 The research flag on the library; the research department removed

## Where it comes from

The person's decision in Chat on Nov 11 (their words kept privately in roadmap/R159.words.md), after both research
projects sent their final findings and wrapped up. They asked whether the department (R131) has a use with an empty
shelf, whether it shares anything across projects, or whether it should go. The lead's answer, which they accepted:
remove it, keep the shared library (R105). Their own addition: keep the research flag as an optional part of the
library. Accepted by the lead in a light form, which they approved.

## Why (what was measured and counted; details in roadmap/R131.md)

- Use: of 12 project plans, one has flagged items (two); those are the only two passes on record. The one curated
  guide was handed over three times, all to the project that wrote it. No project is keeping it now.
- Measured by the research project: research done ahead of a build did not catch what builds later paid for; a
  guide prepared ahead beat the agent's own look-ups on none of four fact-bound tasks; a shelf of topic guides made
  judged designs worse.
- What did hold: untold, agents did not look; and a short line said with the task, at its moment, paid where the
  same words as standing text did not. The flag kept here is that form: the agent judges at planning, colony says
  the line at the item's start, and the agent researches for itself.

## Shape

- Removed: colony/qdept.py, colony/qroom.py, colony/qbranch/ (the core, its prompts, the shipped memory guide), their
  tests, the two settings that belonged to passes (a standing source per project, sourced passes), the item card
  and the guide states on the board, the curated-guide line in `colony research`.
- Kept: `(research)` on an item's line and its `## Research` section; the per-project Research switch; the rule in
  guides/roadmap.md for when to flag, as worded (it was tested and approved); a small badge on a flagged item's row.
- New, in colony/research.py: when a flagged item is marked in progress, colony says one line to its agent, once:
  the item's question (the section's Subject), look in the library, research what it does not cover and read the
  sources themselves, add what another project could reuse. Not quiet: it has to arrive before the design, and an
  agent that has just started an item is at work.
- The part of the flag rule that described colony's pass (about 157 words) is replaced by two lines.
- The memory guide (its living second edition) becomes an ordinary library entry, found by a search, handed to no
  one by word match.
- Records on this machine stay where they are (`qbranch/` in the board's home, and each project's own record): one
  project in progress was pointed at a guide there.

## How it is checked

1. Unit cases: a flagged item going in progress gets the line once, with its question; an unflagged item gets
   nothing; a project with research off gets nothing; a first look says nothing about items already under way; the
   badge shows on a flagged row.
2. Nothing in the tree imports the removed modules; `colony research` and the board's item and settings pages load.
3. The full suite passes before the board restarts; after it, the watcher's log shows no errors from the new check.
4. The memory guide is found by `colony research "agent memory"`.
5. On Dec 9, with the cleaning reading: flagged items since, whether research happened at their start, whether it
   changed anything. Nothing likely or obvious: propose removing the flag too.
