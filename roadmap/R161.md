# R161 The page follows the voice by a switch the person sets

## Where it comes from

The person's idea (Nov 11, two messages in Chat; their words are kept privately in roadmap/R150.words.md). The
page used to follow what was read only where rules allowed it: in Chat, a set number of seconds after their last
touch, not while writing. A sign on the player reported which of those held. Their idea: make that sign a button.
On, the page goes to what is being read wherever they are. Off, the page is never moved. Their second message
added the one rule that is kept: leaving that chat by hand, or going to another project, turns it off, because
they have plainly gone to look at something else.

The first player (R108) had a follow switch, kept in its settings; it followed only inside the chat on screen and
was replaced by three reading modes when following began to cross projects. This is not a return to that one: it
crosses projects, it is on the player's face, and it lets go by itself.

Mine, said to them before building and not objected to: words typed on a page and not sent keep the page where
it is; the map's "keep me on it" mark for the icon; the Return button and the Audio only mode go, since the
switch covers both.

## Shape

- One switch, kept on the device: beside the pill at rest, in the bar while something is read. On by default.
- On: as each part begins the page goes to it. In its own chat, that part and each sentence are brought into
  view; from any other page, that chat is opened in place, so the voice goes on. No wait after a touch.
- Off: nothing moves or marks the page. The sound goes on.
- It turns itself off when the page is changed by hand, while a reply is being read (not paused), to anywhere
  but that reply's chat: another project's chat, or a page that is no chat. A page the reading itself opened does
  not count, nor does a change of page with nothing being read or with the reading paused.
- Tapped on, the page goes at once to what is being read, at the sentence.
- Words typed and not sent on the page hold it there (a box in use, or a box whose text differs from what the
  page came with). The message box that floats over every page is not part of the page and travels with it.
- Settings: "Aloud" or "Text only". "Audio only", where a device still has it chosen, is the switch off; tapping
  the switch on makes it Aloud with follow.
- Removed: the wait and its setting, the in-Chat rule, the sign's six explanations, the Return button and the
  remembered return target, the special case for a reply tapped in History.

## Not built, and why

- A scroll by hand inside the chat being read does not turn it off. The next sentence below the view brings the
  page back. Said to the person as the cost of a plain switch; if it annoys, the fix is to let a scroll switch it
  off, as a map's re-centre does.

## How it is checked

1. Unit cases on the production player (tests/player_writing.js): on shows the part at once and changes nothing
   about the sound; on opens the chat from anywhere, off never moves the page; typed words hold the page; a page
   changed by hand turns it off, its own chat keeps it on; paused or idle, a change of page leaves it on.
2. In a real browser at phone width (three scripts under tests/, run with Chrome mounted): the switch on, off,
   left by hand, kept through a reload, the renamed and the duplicate label, the old Audio only, paused, typed
   words; following from another page through a touch; the sentence in a long reply.
3. The full suite passes before the board restarts.

## What was built, and the check's result

- colony/board.py: the shared module keeps only "a touch stops a scroll the reading began" and whether the page
  is a chat; in-place navigation says of each arrival whether the reading made it; the player keeps the switch
  (`followOn`, `setFollow`, `arrived`) and one `showHere`; the chat page follows wherever the player says follow
  is on. 80 lines added and 134 removed.
- Checks 1 to 3 pass: 1,142 in the suite; the production player's cases; five browser scripts.
- Seen on the way, not from this change and not yet looked into: the browser test of the media viewer fails on
  the unchanged code as well ("Chat scroll preserved"), in the sandbox used here.
