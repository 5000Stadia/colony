# R153 Model data from Artificial Analysis's new free list: only the models colony weighs, no page reading

## The request (2026-11-09)

After learning the 17 unscored model pages were deprecated models, the person asked that deprecated models not be
considered or read at all: start from the exact models colony's provider connections offer, and keep the
intelligence list to those. The chart may show every model behind a switch, as information, with little time
spent on it. Their words are in R153.words.md, kept on this machine, out of the public repository.

## What I found

- The lineup was already the discovered one (each provider's own model list). The four deprecated models were in
  it because Codex still lists them as older models: GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna, and GPT-6 Sol.
- A fault, fixed with this item: Artificial Analysis retired its legacy data address on Nov 4, 2026 (410 Gone).
  Colony's daily fetch failed from then on and said so nowhere, so Auto chose on the Nov 4 reply and never saw
  Fable 5.2 (released Nov 9).
- The replacement free list (`/api/v2/language/models/free`, 200 models a page) names its index version and
  gives each pair its Intelligence Index cost per task. That made the page reading, and the estimates and carried
  token volumes built around its gaps, unnecessary.

## My reading

- Colony weighs a pair only where Artificial Analysis measures it: nothing is estimated or carried.
- A model is left out when a newer version of its own line, from the same program, is scored (GPT-6.1 Sol replaces
  GPT-6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Sol). Until the newer one is scored, the older one stays, so a release never leaves a line
  with nothing weighed. The ones left out stay runnable by name.
- The Models page says which models colony can run and weighs, and which it leaves out. The chart's switch shows
  every other model the list scores, in grey. None of them is weighed.
- The doctor and the Models page say when the data hasn't come for over two days, so a retired address is seen.
