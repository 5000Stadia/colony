# R143 — A small roadmap

A small roadmap: the plan holds the vision, milestones and one short line per item, each naming its 0-3 related items; each item's full text lives in a details file beside it; starting an item, the agent first decides its brief (after R139). The person, 2026-11-01, taking the cleanup pass on colony's ROADMAP.md (gate ga01bde43): "Full agree on making the roadmap read clean, elegent, small. If an agent needs to read the details of a roadmap so be it. The clearest short turse description or even title of items in the past should be sufficient to cleanly orient to present. Future roadmap items possibly too. Each roadmap item probably has 0-3 other roadmap items that might be relevent to that one, or that one relevant to preparing for those. Consideration should be made at first step what a supportive roadmap brief would look like before getting started and not muck up the rest of the context." Agent's reading: finished items become a terse title, open ones a title and a line of why, each with its related items; the full text (their words, readings, checks, findings) moves to roadmap/R#.md, which the board shows on the item's page and the lead's plan commit carries; at an item's start the agent reads its details and its related items', nothing more. Check: board and plan commit support first (tests); colony's own pass, measured by the cleanup check against its 4% of reading; the format and the brief step blind-tested on item starts (tokens and quality against today's long roadmap) before every project's guidance changes.
