# R77 — Every record of the person's words carries the agent's reading

Every record of the person's words carries the agent's reading: colony vision, gate answers and consultant adoptions take a short context note (what was discussed, what the person meant, what it changes) kept beside their words in the history, and agents are told to write it. Agreed 2026-10-02. The person's words: "the original context from the user is necessary but so too is some interpretation within that context … Include any in conversation context summary from you the agent? I suspect that that information arguably may be more important down the road than the literal words from the person which may frankly be less coherent." Then: "Yes". Agent's reading: a literal quote loses its meaning months later; the agent's summary of the surrounding conversation is what lets a later reader act on it, so it is recorded with the words, never instead of them.
