# R99 — One board for the person

One board for the person: every project with what it needs of them under it, answered there; the steward never decides for them; decisions that reach them are only those that truly need them. The person, 2026-10-21: "Ive muted steward because I felt that it was auto approving some things I didnt want it to…. But I was tired of projects gating what seemed to me like “obviously choose the good option” I wonder if steward poses more of a risk… like if I turn it on now when you pass a question to me it may answer on my behalf. I also just wanted one chat interface to engage with what I needed to for each project…. I wonder if an overview of all projects board (like whats on stewards overview right now) with surfacing needs you popping up under each is a better approach", then "Yes full green light trusting your judgement for elegant informative workflow for surfacing decisions that truly should have the human in the loop and sharp discernment if there is a “best” in the values and history shaped by the vision to choose what reaches and adheres to our vision, mission and roadmap best." Agent's reading: (1) the front page becomes every project, waiting ones first, each with its needs answered in place, the steward optional; (2) in code, the steward can only suggest an answer to a decision, never close it; (3) agents decide what has a best answer by the vision, mission and roadmap and say so, bringing only what truly needs the person: a one-tap "Obvious, you decide" on a decision hands it back and teaches the agent where the line is.
