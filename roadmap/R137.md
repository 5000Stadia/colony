# R137 — Waiting costs nothing

Waiting costs nothing: agents wait by being woken, never by sleeping and checking. The person, 2026-10-31, after the Codex burst: "I would like the waiting, checking and answering. To cost no tokens per your suggestion is that something we can bake into colony itself? Even if it is a directive, but preferably if it is an effective tool agents have a clean reflex to use?" Then, on the three parts proposed: "Agreed I haven't really thought through on three, but it is net beneficial or more robust let's go for it seems to add to the clunk lets not." Agent's reading: every check an agent makes while waiting re-sends its whole conversation. Measured on Oct 30-31: Bookflow's Codex leads slept 317 times for 30-60 s and 276 of those woke to no news (Codex's sleep ends early when news comes, so one long sleep would have been one step), and its wait_agent calls used 10 s timeouts, 29 of 36 ending with no news; on Claude, 25 fixed sleep-then-check calls, while its 192 loops that waited inside one command cost one step each and are the right way. Both programs already have waits that wake the agent for free (Claude: a background command's or helper's notice, Monitor; Codex: wait on a running command, wait_agent), so what is missing is the reflex. Two parts: (1) one clause in the guidance every project gets, beside colony wake: waiting on your own work costs nothing when it wakes you, and each sleep-and-check re-reads the whole conversation; (2) a check on each shell command, through each program's PreToolUse hook, that turns back a fixed sleep of 5 s or more outside a loop, with that program's free way to wait; loops that wait inside one command go through; short settling pauses and commands already in the background go through, and the check lets everything through if it fails. Left out: colony wake --until (the board watching a condition), which the person judged clunk unless it proves needed. Check: tests that each program's hook turns back the fixed sleeps seen in the transcripts and lets the loops, short pauses and background ones through, fast enough to sit before every shell command; then the same count re-run on each project a few days after it is live, Bookflow's waiting share near zero. Built and live 2026-10-31 (377e2ea): the full suite passes (1039 tests); the check answers in about 55 ms through `colony context --hook wait`, is written into each project's settings when colony wires it (board start, restart, the steward's daily pass), and a running Claude session took it up at once (a `sleep 5 && …` in the lead's own session was turned back with the message). Codex consoles get it at their next start; Codex's own sleep tool is left unhooked (a long one is a free wait). The re-count is set as a wake for Nov 3.
