# R151 Playback steps part by part

## The request (2026-11-08)

The person asked whether skip could step through the smaller groups a reply already shows (the prose
between its runs of commands) instead of the whole reply, and for a back button to go back and forth. Their words are in R151.words.md, kept on this machine, out of the public repository.

## My reading

- Each reply already reaches the player as parts: one per stretch of prose between the runs of commands that Chat
  folds to a line ("Ran 6 commands"). Skip (⏭, in the bar and on the lock screen) now skips every part left in the
  reply. It should go to the next part instead, and past a reply's last part, on to the next reply.
- A back button (⏮) beside it goes to the start of the part before. From a reply's first part, it goes back to the
  last part of the reply before, as History already holds it. Pressed early in a part, it goes back a part; later in a
  part, it restarts that part, as music players do. The lock screen's previous-track gets the same move.
- A part stepped past counts as skipped, as now; one gone back to plays again.
- Documents keep their sections: skip and back step section by section.

## Check

- Tests on the player's script, where the board's tests already reach it: skip moves one part; past the last, the
  next reply; back restarts a part or goes to the one before, across a reply's start; a document's sections are
  unchanged.
- Live: a real multi-part reply on the person's board, played in the browser, skipping and going back through its
  parts; then on a phone, including the lock screen.

## Built and checked (2026-11-09, c88e72a)

- ⏭ steps one part; past the last, the next reply; the part stepped past is marked skipped (only if still in the
  line). ⏮ sits left of ❚❚: in a part's first clip and its first 3 seconds (the project's name, where read first,
  counts as early), it goes to the part before, and from a reply's first part to the last part of the reply this page
  read before it; later, it restarts the part. The lock screen's previous-track calls the same move. Documents step
  section by section both ways.
- The reply before comes from what this page read, newest last: up to 20, for 12 hours, kept by their keys in the
  page's own storage, so their words are fetched again after a reload. The reading gone back from waits and comes
  again from its start after the one gone back to. A History reply, a ▷ reply, and Play and Clear each fit in: Play
  starts afresh, and a cleared reply doesn't come again.
- Tests: six scenarios in tests/player_writing.js, run against both the board's voice and the phone's (222 passes).
  Each fails on the old player. Full suite passes (1263).
- Live, on the person's board (headless Chrome at 390px, light and dark, sound stubbed, Auto off): a History run of
  two replies stepped through its 9 parts with ⏭ and into the next reply. ⏮ at that reply's start went to the last
  part of the one before, ⏮ again to the part before that, and ⏭ past the end came back to the reply it had left.
  No page errors, and nothing scrolls sideways.
- Seen while checking: a History replay of a turn still growing includes parts still waiting in the line, so
  stepping past one marks it skipped, as the old skip did. That's right when the person does it. My test marked two
  of their waiting parts (one Bookflow, one Memory Research), and I took those two rows out of heard.jsonl (backups in
  the session scratchpad). A live check should block /chat/heard in its page.
- Left for the person: the phone and its lock screen (⏮ and ⏭ there), which only their iPhone can show.
