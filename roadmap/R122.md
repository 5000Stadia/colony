# R122 — Timed wakes

Timed wakes: `colony wake [NAME] --at TIME | --in DURATION [--every DURATION] "why"` lands as a note in the project's own conversation when it's due. It is listed and cancellable on the project's page, kept across restarts and reboots, costs no tokens until it fires, waits for an idle moment and the person's active hours, and works the same on Claude and Codex. It replaces a cron job or a sleeping process no one can see. The person, 2026-10-28: "Time to wake sounds certainly helpful. I can imagine a ton of projects that need a timed wake to check on something periodically". Of the other nine lessons from the predecessors' study (design/predecessors/), they said: "a lot of accounting for almost no gain to try to resolve problems that we haven't seen yet", so those are dropped. Built small, and whether agents reach for it is watched: KERNOS built follow-ups and its live data shows none was ever used. Built and live 2026-10-28 (da70b26): `colony wake [NAME] --at|--in [--every] "why"`, `--list`, `--cancel ID`; kept in .board/wakes.jsonl (made, fired, cancelled, so use can be counted); fired by the watcher's own pass as a note delivered with a prompt, only when the console is idle and inside active hours, once after any downtime; listed with Cancel on the Overview; one line in the project instructions.
