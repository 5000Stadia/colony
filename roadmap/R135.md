(1) A button at the band's left, always in reach, opens a list of every project: each with its state, what it's at, what waits on the person and its nest named. The choice is kept on the device. It replaces the ☰ All projects link, and the list ends with a link to that page. The one that stirred last is at the left, and a box slides into the left as its project starts work. No nests and no slide-outs in the band. Anything pinned still comes first, as the person's own choice (none is pinned today). The desk sidebar follows the same model, a flat list in the same order with the same button, so the two never disagree.
