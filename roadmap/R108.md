# R108 — A floating player for reading aloud, over every page of the board

A floating player for reading aloud, over every page of the board. The person, 2026-10-26: "Can I have the voice portion of playback pop up a bottom floating player with the play controls that lives over different sections of colony? I also want it to have a pull down of the queued sections - timestamp and a few words preview so user can skip down to right area. Skipping to “right” area should auto flag previous as heard. Also a toggle for visual focus on this part of chat and scrolling to that part per section when Im on the same page. If I go to another project chat that has auto play while its still playing THEN it should stop the previous chat queue, leaving, but hasn’t played as unheard for resume when they go back to that chat. Then the new chat I’m entering should take focus." Agent's reading: the board's pages are whole page loads, which stop the sound, so the reading moves out of the chat into the board's frame, and while something is being read (or paused mid-reading) a link or a form changes the page in place rather than loading a new one, so the sound goes on; at any other time pages load as they do now. A section is one part of a reply as it was written (the conversation's own pieces, each with its time). The list shows the sections of this reading: heard ones ticked, the one playing, those waiting; a tap plays from there, those skipped counted heard. Follow-in-chat (a toggle kept on the device): on the chat being read, the section being heard is marked and brought into view as each starts. Entering another project's chat that would read by itself takes the voice: the first project's reading is kept, paused, from where it stopped, as Continue on its chat and on the player. Check: in headless Chrome against a board on a spare port, reading goes on across a page change by link and by form, the list jumps and marks the skipped heard, follow marks and scrolls, and a second chat with something new takes over while the first keeps its place. Built: the bar shows the project and the words being read with pause, skip and stop; its list of sections (time, first words, heard ticked) plays from a tap, those before it counted heard; Follow in chat on by default; the page dims while the next loads in place; after a reload the latest kept reading shows paused on any page. Checked in headless Chrome at phone width, 26 checks passing.
