# R89 — The showcase

The showcase: each project's lab-style page of deliverables worth seeing. An entry says what it is and how to see or try it; newest first, then Earlier, so it is the project's history of what was shown. The person may rate and comment, never must; what they say reaches the agent as direction. Agents add an entry only when seeing it tells the person something about quality or direction. Modelled on holo-emitter's lab/index.html. Same words as R88.
