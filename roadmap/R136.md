# R136 — Active states drawn apart, helpers as dots, and the band ordered by them

Active states drawn apart, helpers as dots, and the band ordered by them. The person, 2026-10-31, on seeing an idle-looking box left of one whose dot spun: "Can we have that if an active project goes idle it bumps below all active projects as well", then "But i see right now in the horizontal bar a project with no animation to the left of one animated active", then "Oh then maybe we just need more thourough animations for active states", "Would be cool to show colored dots to indicate helpers and subagents and their status" and "Cool to have subagent status dots right under the text newline in a box". Agent's reading: the dot counted a project at work while only helpers or background commands ran behind an idle screen, but the order read the screen alone (Bookflow: 5 helpers and 34 shells behind an idle screen). Fixed so the order follows the dot. Then three active states drawn apart: mid-turn (the spinning ring, and a stripe running along the box's foot), background only (a slow dotted ring), and idle. The band orders them mid-turn first, then background only, then idle, each by when it last stirred, so leftover background work never takes the left from an agent mid-turn. Each helper at work is a dot on a line under its project's name, coloured by tier (step-up, routine, chores, rote, other), pulsing while its tokens move, and a hollow amber ring after 15 minutes without, which means it is waiting on something long or stuck; past six, +N. The list carries the key. Also: "as much as i like the rainbow animation button on skip it doesnt make sense anymore, lets ditch the animation": the player's Skip loses its rainbow ring. Check: tests for the three states' order, the dot classes, helper dots (tier, quiet after 15 minutes still, +N) in the band and the status, and no ring on Skip; a look on a test board at phone and desk widths.
