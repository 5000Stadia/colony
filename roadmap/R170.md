# R170 — One link to open on every card waiting on the person

The person's idea (2026-11-18; their words in R170.words.md, private). A card waiting on them (a question in chat,
a decision, a sign-off) often refers to something to look at without a way to get there: they ask again, or hunt
for a link that may be stale. Their framing: a link board the project keeps, which either of them can add to; a card
that refers to something points at its entry there. No new rules in agents' context.

## What it measured before design

- The week's friction reading (Nov 11 to 18, roadmap/R146.md) counted 8 cases in three projects of something shown
  that the person could not open or find.
- Sign-offs mostly carry a link already: 72 of the week's 75 had an address in their words. The gap is a question
  asked in chat (the person's example: a project's "on the live site now, reload to see them", with no link), a
  decision, and an address buried in a long text.
- An address written in a card is already a link to tap (Nov 14), and a project file or folder named there opens too.
- Colony already knows each project's live addresses (`colony serve`), and starts a project's server when a link
  to it is tapped and nothing answers (R162).
- The Library is where links are kept today; one project's only link there was an old address on the home network,
  not the live one its cards used.

## Shape

1. **The link board is the project's Library.** Its live addresses show there by themselves, from what colony serves
   for it, above what either side pinned. Nothing to keep current by hand.
2. **Every card gets one Open.** A sign-off, decision or question in chat opens the link its agent gave, else the
   first address in its words, else the Showcase entry or Library link its words name by title (a decision that
   named a page on its board by its title now opens that page). A card that names none opens the
   project's live address, or its Library where it serves several. Agents learn nothing new for it.
3. **A deeper page is asked for at the one moment it matters.** `colony ready` and `colony gate` take `--open`, a URL
   or the title of a Showcase entry or Library link, and each says in its reply which link the card will open, and how to point it at a
   deeper page. Said then, never as a standing rule.

Decided: a link given with `--open` stays on its card, not pinned. Pinning every deep link would fill the Library
with pages of finished items; the board keeps what lasts.

## How it will be checked

- Tests: each card kind opens what its words name, else what was given, else the live address; none where there is
  none; the Library shows live addresses; `--open` by title and by URL; the commands' replies.
- Replay: last week's cards across projects, how many get an Open that leads somewhere.
- Live: the friction readings' count of things the person could not open or find (8 in the week to Nov 18).

## What was built (2026-11-18)

- `board.card_link` chooses what a card opens; `open_html` draws its one Open (sign-offs, decisions, questions in
  chat); the Library heads with a Live group from `serving.served`; `colony ready` and `colony gate` take `--open`
  and say in their reply what the card opens. A Showcase entry or Library link is kept on the card by its id.
- Tests: `test_every_card_opens_one_link_its_own_else_the_live_site`. Suite 1,175 pass.
- Replay over the week's 142 sign-offs and decisions (Nov 11 to 18): 72 open an address in their words, 5 a board
  entry or link they name, 14 the project's live site, 23 the Library of a project serving two addresses, and 28
  nothing: all 28 in projects that serve nothing, mostly decisions with nothing to look at.
- The card from the person's Inbox when it was built, a decision naming a page on its board, opens that page.
- Still to read: the friction readings' count of things the person could not open or find (8 to Nov 18), from the
  reading of Nov 25.
