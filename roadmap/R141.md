# R141 — Questions in flight only for the unforeseen

Questions in flight only for the unforeseen: when the vision and the plan were aligned at design time, a decision with a clear best answer from them is decided, not asked. The person, 2026-11-01: "with an improvement on vision alignment at design time there should be less questions in flight if the prospective decision had a clear best answer to match the vision or roadmap description. We want to have a bit of tested discernment here if the needs you is a valuable clarification as it comes up or redundant reclarification." Their picture: a managing director and a writer agree a shared vision, and if it is good enough the final is "so is this it?", with a review that may bring minor unforeseen changes; "What we dont want is a conversation and then along the way 'like this?' 'How about this?' 'Im going to do this ok?'" Or a wedding planner: "The plan is before the execution and if its good theres few questions only for ubforseen circumstances on inplementation day". Agent's reading: the measure is the share of in-flight questions that are valuable (unforeseen, theirs, not answered by the vision, the plan or an earlier decision) against redundant ones (answered there, reversible and minor, or asking leave: "I'm going to do X, ok?"); missing a question that was theirs (costly, leaves their hands, taste with no anchor) counts against as well. R140's tests saw agents end replies on questions often, and the finishing line it added raised that; this asks which of those questions should exist at all. Check: (1) real evidence: the questions agents put to the person on the board over the last weeks, each judged blind as valuable or redundant against the vision and roadmap of its project at the time; (2) a blind test of mid-build moments in a project whose vision and plan are aligned: decisions the vision answers, ones the roadmap or an earlier gate answers, minor reversible ones, genuinely unforeseen ones that are theirs, and the end of an item; scored on redundant asks, missed asks and the work, current guidance against any sharpened wording; ships a change only where it cuts redundant asks without missing the ones that are theirs.
