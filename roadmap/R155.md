# R155 Are colony, the business project and the books project growing into one another? A report, not a plan

Asked by the person on 2026-11-10 (their words: `R155.words.md`, kept on this machine).

## My reading

The person sees three of their projects (colony; their business project, which builds workflow software for local
business owners; and their books project) becoming adjacent in spirit, and thinks they may be able to work together
in ways that help each. They asked for a conversation between the three about it and a report on two questions: is
this the right thing to be considering, and is it feasible. They were explicit about the limit: the report plans
nothing. No convergence roadmap, no items in any project, and existing roadmap items are left out of the reasoning.
This item is the only one, and it is for the report itself.

## The method they asked for

Two assessments, each written without sight of the other, then joined:

1. The business project's agent writes its own assessment and report.
2. Colony's lead writes its own, blind to that one.
3. Colony's lead joins the two: where they agree, where they differ and why, and what neither could settle; then
   presents the result to the person in colony's Chat.

My addition, the person's to reverse: the books project is told the conversation has begun and invited to add its
own view when it next takes a turn. It is in its weekly safe pause, so nothing wakes it for this; its view joins the
report when it comes.

## How it will be checked

- Each assessment exists as a file dated before its author read the other.
- The joined report names every point on which the two differ, and does not smooth one over.
- Nothing in any of the three projects' roadmaps changes because of it, this item apart.
- The person has read the joined report in Chat.

## Where it lives

The assessments and the joined report describe the other projects' own material, so they stay on this machine
(`design/convergence/`, which the repository ignores). This file carries only the shape of the work.
