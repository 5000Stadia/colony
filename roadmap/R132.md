# R132 — A bar for a creative item, picked by the person from real examples

A bar for a creative item, picked by the person from real examples. The person's idea, relayed by Quartermaster (m533471c3), for colony's roadmap-writing step: for creative and game items, a cheap pass finds two or three comparable examples of the finished quality, and the person picks one as the item's bar ("meets or exceeds this"). Agent's reading: it carries the vision's "a real example the person admires" down to single items, as GUIDE's Review section already asks where quality is in the person's eye ("the best real example they would hold it to"); the new part is the agent offering candidates to pick from. Its risk is imitation: R26's principals borrowed whatever was in front of them, so each example names the one aspect it sets the bar for, and is kept as a link, never copied in. Untested; tried against the plain setup before any guidance changes. Then the person, 2026-10-31, on a related thought (a shared picture of done for an item before building, from the three.js environments, where the look was not agreed before the agent took the wheel): "I think for the quality guidance, you are correct and it isn't really a new rule. I think we may want the slightest modification to emphasize that if the deliverable of the roadmap is an identifiable, new media object where quality would be seen by the user from a lens of taste or artistry such as music, visual mediums, or even writing style for either fiction or even something like marketing, taste, style, and a felt vibe of what is being delivered is something that will be difficult to articulate with text and the user should be encouraged to produce at least one item comparable in quality and taste". Agent's reading: one sentence in every project's instructions, beside "Where only the person can judge": for an item that makes a new piece of media judged by taste (music, images, a writing voice in fiction or marketing), ask the person before building for at least one real example of the quality and taste they want, kept as a link that sets the bar for the qualities they name, not a model to copy. The agent offering candidates stays as GUIDE's existing fallback (contrasting sketches when they cannot name one); no new mechanism. Check: the sentence is in the template and the projects' instructions after the board rewrites them. Then the person, on the draft: "I don't want The automatic default to pause the entire roadmap because of insistence to check in with the user before going forward. If the road map item is something like "fix the broke thing so it's not broke" We don't need to have a pause and a Kumbaya. If it is the first big build to apply shaders and the first batch of textures without any description of stylization, then arguably, we should see that good cannot be attained. because good is in the alignment of specific detailed and discussed style, which has not occurred between the user and the project agent, so this would prompt a very reasonable discussion". Agent's reading: the trigger is the missing alignment, not the medium: an item that first sets a look, sound or voice whose style the person has not described or shown. Only that item waits; the rest of the roadmap goes on, and an agreed style carries to later items. The sentence as written: "Where an item first sets a look, sound or voice (shaders and textures, music, a fiction or marketing voice) whose style the person hasn't yet described or shown, good can't be reached until you align on it, since taste is hard to put in words: ask them for at least one real example of the quality and taste they want, kept as a link that sets the bar for what they name in it, not a model to copy. Only that item waits, and an agreed style carries to later ones." Built and live 2026-10-31 (0002898): the full suite passes (1039 tests); after the restart the sentence is in colony's and Bookflow's CLAUDE.md and colony-codex's AGENTS.md.
