# R163 A message to one project can be copied to others, or sent to a whole nest

## Where it comes from

The person asked (Chat, Nov 12; their words are kept privately in roadmap/R163.words.md) for a way to Cc a second
project on a message they send, with each copy saying it was also sent to the others, and, in a second message
while it was being built, for the option to message an entire nest in the same clean way. My reading: when two
agents are told the same thing separately, each tends to write to the other to pass it on; a line in the message
itself, saying who else has it, makes that unnecessary. Nothing here asks an agent to do anything new.

## Shape

- **Cc**, in the message box that floats over every page while Auto reads with text: a small Cc beside To opens the
  list of the other projects; a tap adds one, another tap takes it off, and the list stays open for more. The choice
  is kept with the draft and let go once sent. A project the message is then addressed to is dropped from Cc.
- **A whole nest**: each nest with two or more projects on the board is a row at the end of the To list. Chosen, the
  box reads "To: NAME nest"; its members are no longer offered as copies.
- **What each gets**:
  - the project the message is written to: the words in its console, as ever, under a first line naming who has a
    copy;
  - a Cc'd project: the same words as the person's own note (what Tell projects sends, so a paused project hears it
    when resumed and nothing depends on its console being free), under a line saying it is a copy, who it was
    written to and who else has one;
  - a nest's members: each the person's note, under a line naming the nest and the others who have it.
  No copy goes unless the message itself does: refused at the console, it comes back to the box with its Cc.
- **Tell projects** (front page): with several ticked, each note now carries the same kind of line; with one, the
  words alone, as before.
- A file attached is kept with the project the message is written to (a nest's first), and its path is in every
  copy.

## Not built, and why

- Cc in a Chat page's own box (the one shown with Auto off or reading aloud alone): it has no To line. Tell
  projects, on the front page, sends to several from there.
- A nest as a Cc, or two nests at once: nothing asked for it.
- A nest's archived projects and those off the board are not reached, as with Tell projects.
- The nests the box offers are read when a page loads in full; one made or changed since shows after a reload.

## How it is checked

1. Unit cases (tests/test_board.py, `TellProjectsTest`): the three kinds of copy and their first lines; no copy to
   the one it is written to, to an unknown key, or twice; nothing copied when the message is refused; no Cc, the
   words as written; a nest reaches each member on the board once, with a member named in Cc getting no second
   copy; an unknown nest is refused; a nest of one is not offered; several ticked in Tell projects.
2. In a phone-sized browser against a test board (the same class, gated on the browser fixture): Cc picked beside
   To, two added with the list staying open, one taken off, the head line staying on the screen, the addressee
   changed to a Cc'd project, what is posted, the box cleared after; then a nest chosen as To and what is posted.
3. The suite passes before the board restarts.

## What was built, and the check's result

- colony/board.py: `copies_line`, `cc_roots`, `nest_roots`, `nests_for_box`; `cc` and `nest` on the send route;
  `tell_projects`; the message box (Cc button, the nest rows, what it posts); two style rules.
- Check 1: two new tests and the Tell test extended; the suite passes (1,146).
- Check 2: passes. It found one fault before release: redrawing the list after a pick made the tap count as one
  outside the box, which closed the list after the first choice; a pick is now marked in place.
- Not checked: on the person's phone; a real Cc between two live projects (the first real one is the person's).
