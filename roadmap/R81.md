# R81 — Turbo

Turbo: when a program's weekly usage runs behind pace, colony turns projects up so the week isn't wasted. From day 2 of the window, expected use is the elapsed share of the week, aiming for 99% by the hour before the reset; more than 5 points behind turns turbo on for that program's projects, and catching up turns it off. Each project ticks what turbo means for it: stronger models, going deeper on its queued work (preparation, tests, reviews, prior art, parallel helpers on separate files), and research on a topic typed in a box, encouraged daily. Only projects with queued work, or research ticked, are turned up. Never noise: work worth doing, or the agent says there's none and stops. Agreed 2026-10-07. The person's words: "only projects with queued work … ticked options for what turbo mode means including research … a small text box I can type what it would even be researching … the determination should be made after the 2nd day … if we are more than 5% less than that, then we turn on turbo mode … in the last two days the optimal success of this automation is that at the exact hour before the reset we get to 99% or 100% … as long as we are not generating literal nothing or noise or garbage we should be doing some work because we are about to waste the tokens anyways." Agent's reading: unused subscription usage is sunk, so spending it on useful work within each project's agreed scope fits "no extra spend without substantial gain"; model changes apply only at a helper's start or an idle reload, so turbo never disrupts work in progress. Added the same day: when turbo turns on, and at most daily after, the monitor (unless muted) glances at the turbo projects and suggests research where gains haven't been considered, as its own suggestion (colony suggest), never the person's word. The person: "It may be helpful for monitor to add a glance look at the projects and make a nondeterministic intelligence decision for what might receive gains that have not been considered in these times and make the suggestion for the research."
