# R167 — Active hours removed

Nothing is held by the clock any more. A question or decision for the person goes to their list when it is made, at
any hour; agents hear no line about the hour; an agent's own wake fires when it is due.

## Where it came from

Active hours (R82, 2026-10-07) held what agents asked outside a set range and released it together when the range
began, told agents on each turn at night not to end on a question, and kept their wakes for the day. The person
questioned it on 2026-11-15 and, after the count below, decided to take it out (gate gc82fee43). My reading of why:
the two jobs it was built for are done elsewhere now (the guides tell agents not to ask what has an obvious answer;
an idle project with work it can start is told to carry on), and the Inbox is the right shape for what needs them,
for posting and for answering, whatever the hour.

The count, from every project's records over the 40 nights the feature ran: 51 decisions were held, on 17
mornings, mostly one to three at a time. By day, many answers took as long to come as a night does: the clock was
a weak guide to when an answer comes. Turns ended on a question
about a quarter as often at night as by day, but the share was already lower at night before the feature and the
guides changed in the same weeks, so the records could not show the night line caused it.

## What changed

- The `active_hours` setting, its field in Settings and its line in the guidance every project reads are gone.
- Gates and questions are no longer stamped to wait for a time; the "from outside your hours" list is gone.
- Wakes fire at any hour once their console is idle.
- The person's day (a project's daily page, the day's decisions numbered) runs midnight to midnight, as it did
  with the setting off.
- A paused project still holds its decisions until it resumes: that is the pause's own, and stays.

## How it was checked (2026-11-15)

- Tests (tests.test_board.NoHoursTest and the wake, Inbox and daily-page tests changed with it): a gate or a
  question made at 3 AM is the person's at once and its version stops for it as by day; a turn at night hears
  nothing about the hour; a due wake fires at night once its console is idle; the setting is refused as unknown
  and an old board's saved value is dropped; a hold the old feature wrote holds nothing; a paused project's
  decisions still come back as new on resume. The whole suite passes.
- Live: Settings shows no hours field and `colony settings` no such line; every project on the board had the
  sentence about asking inside the hours taken out of the guidance it reads at the restart.

One thing that moved with it: an Inbox card set aside until tomorrow, or until a date, comes back at midnight,
where it came back as the hours began.

Not yet seen: a night with it gone.
