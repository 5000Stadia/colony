# R152 Tell projects, then the steward retired

## The request (2026-11-08 and 09)

The person asked to decommission the steward unless it gives a unique value worth keeping, and if it does,
to talk about how that value is held. They said yes on gate ge39f386d (Nov 9): build the box first, then remove the
steward. Their words are in R152.words.md, kept on this machine, out of the public repository.

## My reading

- The one thing only the steward did was carry the person's words to several projects at once, as theirs. Colony
  does the rest now: safe and week pauses, waking at resets, idle nudges, status reports (R150), answering from
  Needs you, and recovery. Bookflow's resume on Nov 8 showed the gap: their answer, given on colony's board, could
  only reach Bookflow as mail quoting them.
- The box: on the board's main page, write once, tick one project, several or all, and send. Each project gets it
  as the person's own note, the same as one written on its page, so it carries their authority and reaches the agent
  by itself. Nothing is sent to a project left unticked.
- Then the steward goes: its session, brief, page, settings, CLI commands and the guidance that mentions it. Colony's
  watcher stays, since it already runs without the steward.
- The vision's monitor sentence changes with it: colony watches for the person, and their word reaches one project
  or many at once.

## Check

## The box: built and checked (2026-11-09, c53f540)

- On the front page, folded under "Tell projects": a text box, a tick for each project (paused ones marked) and
  "Every project", and Send. Each ticked project gets the words as the person's own note on the whole project
  (`add_note(root, None, text, author='person')`, the same as their note on its page). The page then says who was
  told. No words or no ticks sends nothing. Projects are picked by folder, not by their place in the list, so a project
  added meanwhile can't shift a tick onto the wrong one.
- Tests: two in TellProjectsTest (the ticked get it and no other; the route through a served board). Full suite
  passes (1265).
- Live: the box on the person's board at 390px, light and dark, with all 14 projects listed and nothing sent (the
  page's post was blocked).
