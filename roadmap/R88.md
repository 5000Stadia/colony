Full words in design/vision-alignment.md (local). Agent's reading: the vision directs the work, and the person is its guide; beyond what the work is, it holds why they want it, where it lands (who, how many, how found, on what terms), the edge they bring, and what using it should feel like; how involved they are is theirs to set, and nothing waits on their sign-off. Day one asks for these. New projects get it; a running one takes it up when its vision is next revised. Tested before it goes live on three made-up briefs, today's wording against the new, each graded blind against the persona's hidden answers.
