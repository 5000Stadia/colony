# R164 Each Inbox card can be read aloud, in its project's voice

## Where it comes from

The person asked for it (Chat, Nov 12; their words are kept privately in roadmap/R164.words.md): a button on each
Inbox item that plays it aloud in the voice of the project it belongs to. My reading: the Inbox is where they judge
what waits on them, they listen to the board more than they read it, and a card was the one place a waiting item
could not be heard (a decision in a project's Chat already had its own play button).

## Shape

- **▷ on the card**, at the end of its head line. A tap sends the card's words to the board's player, which reads
  them in the project's voice: the one it was given, else the colony's. On the card being read the button is ❚❚ (a
  tap holds it) and then ▷ again (a tap goes on from where it stopped), as under a reply in Chat. The player's bar
  does the rest.
- **What is read** (`inbox.spoken`): all the card says. Whose it is, what kind of thing and how long it has waited;
  its deadline if it has one; the item it belongs to, by its name; then what the card puts to the person. A
  decision: the last thing the person asked on it and what they were told, its question, why, a default that is not
  among the choices, each point to accept or reject, and the choices, the recommended one said so. A sign-off: what
  is ready and how to check it. A question asked in a console: the turn that asked it. A version to review: its
  outcome, definition and check. A console that needs them: only that it does (a screen is not for the ear).
  The first version read a decision's question and why alone; the person found a card with four points read only to
  its first paragraph (Chat, Nov 12, the second message in roadmap/R164.words.md), and the reading was widened the
  same day. A decision's ▷ in a project's Chat reads the same.
- Left to the page: buttons and links, and the line under each point saying which consultant raised it.
- Prepared as a reply is for the voice: no code, a link as its words, a web address as "the link", a path as its
  last name, a roadmap number followed by its name the first time.
- The reading has no page to follow to, so the Inbox stays where it is while it is read, whatever the follow switch
  says. The message box, where it floats, addresses the project being read, as with any reading.

## Not built, and why

- A button on each row of the whole list (See all). The request was for the Inbox's items, which come one a card;
  the same words would serve there if it is wanted.
- Reading the next card by itself after an answer. Each is a tap.

## How it is checked

1. Unit (tests/test_board.py, `InboxTest` and the Chat ▷ test): the words for a decision, its points and its last
   exchange, a sign-off and a deadline; the button on the
   card with the project's own voice, and the colony's where it has none.
2. In a phone-sized browser against a test board (gated on `COLONY_BROWSER_TEST=1`): a tap starts the player on that
   card, the clip is asked for in the project's voice, the card's last words are asked for too, the Inbox stays, a
   second tap holds and a third goes on; the
   button is thumb-sized and inside the card.
3. The suite passes before the board restarts; the live Inbox carries the button with the top card's real words.

## What was built, and the check's result

- colony/inbox.py (`spoken`, `play_button`, `PLAY`), colony/board.py (`no_addresses`, taken out of `gate_spoken` so
  both use it, and made to drop a path's leading slash and trailing full stop; the button's style).
- Checks 1 to 3 pass (the suite: 1,148). Nov 12, the live Inbox's top card carried the button with its project's
  voice and its sign-off's words.
- After the widening: every live card's visible lines were compared with its reading; what is left unread is the
  buttons, link labels and consultant lines above. The suite and the browser check pass again.
- Not checked: heard on the person's phone by me (the person heard the first version there).

## Two pages of the board alive on one phone (Nov 12)

The person reported, in Chat, that playback had gone wrong (their words in roadmap/R164.words.md). What their
phone's own record showed (`~/.config/colony/chat-measure.log`, from 23:48 UTC): every resume was let play and
stopped by the phone within the same second, on each page opened; and an older page of the board, hidden for 31
minutes and still running, had its silence stopped at the same moments a freshly opened page started. No player
code had changed that day (the diff since the item before this one leaves the player alone).

Reading: two pages of the board were alive on the phone at once, a tab still reading while hidden and the board
opened again in another. A phone plays one tab's sound at a time, so a start in one stops the other's, and what it
stopped it may set going again by itself. Nothing told the pages about each other. The same gap on a desk: two
pages with a reading each read at once (the new check fails on the old player for that reason).

What changed (colony/board.py, the player):

- A page in view that starts a sound says so to the board's other pages on that device. A hidden page that hears it
  goes quiet, held as if the phone had stopped it, and stays quiet until the person comes to it (it is in view
  again, they touch it, or they press play for it on the lock screen).
- A sound a page did not ask for, set going while it is held, is stopped at once.
- Found on the way: with Auto on, a tap on a held reading's own button (a card's, a reply's, a question's) took it
  up on the press and held it again on the click that followed. The press now leaves it to the button.

Checks: a browser test with two pages on a test board (the hidden one goes quiet when the other reads; what is
set going there without the page asking stops; one tap on the held reading's button reads it; back on the first
page, it reads and the other goes quiet), which fails on the player as it was; the other player browser tests; the
suite (1,152).

Not checked: on the person's phone. Whether the phone's own stop-and-restart between tabs is exactly as read
above is not proven from here; the record fits it and the check covers both ways a hidden page could sound again
(by its own code, or set going from outside).

## Correction: the cause was the phone's own audio, not two pages (Nov 12, evening)

The reading in the section above was wrong as the cause. The person closed Safari completely and the failure stayed,
so no second page was involved. A temporary measure in the player (`phone-stop` lines in
`~/.config/colony/chat-measure.log`) then showed the phone stopping both of the page's sounds at the same instant, 40
to 370 ms after each start, with nothing or a tenth of a second played, sometimes twice more within a few
milliseconds. None of the day's commits before the first failure touched the player. The person restarted the phone
and reading worked at once: the next page's record has only pauses of their own. So the phone's audio had got into a
state where it stopped every sound a page started; what put it there is not known (the last good reading ended 3:08 PM,
dictation held a reading at 3:12 and 3:17, the first failure was 3:48).

What follows from it:

- The one-page-has-the-sound change was built on the wrong reading and was never heard on a phone. It was taken out
  the same evening (lead's decision), leaving the fix for a reading's own button resuming and holding again in one
  tap, which is a fault read from the code; the browser test was cut down to that case.
- The `phone-stop` measure stays until the friction reading of Nov 18 and comes out then if the failure has not
  returned; if it returns, its lines are the first thing to read.
- `/lab/sound` stays: four taps on a page with none of the player, which tell a phone that refuses any sound from the
  board from a fault in the player's way of starting one. It was built during this and not needed in the end, since
  the restart answered first.
- Not built, to be proposed: when the phone stops several starts in a row within half a second, the bar could say so
  and that restarting the phone has cleared it before. Seen once.
