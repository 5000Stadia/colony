# R164 Each Inbox card can be read aloud, in its project's voice

## Where it comes from

The person asked for it (Chat, Nov 12; their words are kept privately in roadmap/R164.words.md): a button on each
Inbox item that plays it aloud in the voice of the project it belongs to. My reading: the Inbox is where they judge
what waits on them, they listen to the board more than they read it, and a card was the one place a waiting item
could not be heard (a decision in a project's Chat already had its own play button).

## Shape

- **▷ on the card**, at the end of its head line. A tap sends the card's words to the board's player, which reads
  them in the project's voice: the one it was given, else the colony's. On the card being read the button is ❚❚ (a
  tap holds it) and then ▷ again (a tap goes on from where it stopped), as under a reply in Chat. The player's bar
  does the rest.
- **What is read** (`inbox.spoken`): all the card says. Whose it is, what kind of thing and how long it has waited;
  its deadline if it has one; the item it belongs to, by its name; then what the card puts to the person. A
  decision: the last thing the person asked on it and what they were told, its question, why, a default that is not
  among the choices, each point to accept or reject, and the choices, the recommended one said so. A sign-off: what
  is ready and how to check it. A question asked in a console: the turn that asked it. A version to review: its
  outcome, definition and check. A console that needs them: only that it does (a screen is not for the ear).
  The first version read a decision's question and why alone; the person found a card with four points read only to
  its first paragraph (Chat, Nov 12, the second message in roadmap/R164.words.md), and the reading was widened the
  same day. A decision's ▷ in a project's Chat reads the same.
- Left to the page: buttons and links, and the line under each point saying which consultant raised it.
- Prepared as a reply is for the voice: no code, a link as its words, a web address as "the link", a path as its
  last name, a roadmap number followed by its name the first time.
- The reading has no page to follow to, so the Inbox stays where it is while it is read, whatever the follow switch
  says. The message box, where it floats, addresses the project being read, as with any reading.

## Not built, and why

- A button on each row of the whole list (See all). The request was for the Inbox's items, which come one a card;
  the same words would serve there if it is wanted.
- Reading the next card by itself after an answer. Each is a tap.

## How it is checked

1. Unit (tests/test_board.py, `InboxTest` and the Chat ▷ test): the words for a decision, its points and its last
   exchange, a sign-off and a deadline; the button on the
   card with the project's own voice, and the colony's where it has none.
2. In a phone-sized browser against a test board (gated on `COLONY_BROWSER_TEST=1`): a tap starts the player on that
   card, the clip is asked for in the project's voice, the card's last words are asked for too, the Inbox stays, a
   second tap holds and a third goes on; the
   button is thumb-sized and inside the card.
3. The suite passes before the board restarts; the live Inbox carries the button with the top card's real words.

## What was built, and the check's result

- colony/inbox.py (`spoken`, `play_button`, `PLAY`), colony/board.py (`no_addresses`, taken out of `gate_spoken` so
  both use it, and made to drop a path's leading slash and trailing full stop; the button's style).
- Checks 1 to 3 pass (the suite: 1,148). Nov 12, the live Inbox's top card carried the button with its project's
  voice and its sign-off's words.
- After the widening: every live card's visible lines were compared with its reading; what is left unread is the
  buttons, link labels and consultant lines above. The suite and the browser check pass again.
- Not checked: heard on the person's phone by me (the person heard the first version there).
