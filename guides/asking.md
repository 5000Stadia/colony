# Putting a decision or question to the person

Foresee the decisions an item will need and ask them together. A choice with an obvious best is not one to ask:
take it and say so. What you do ask waits for their answer: nothing stands because they skipped it.
A question that matters only later waits for its moment.

Something to weigh that is not yet a decision (real options, a shape that could go more than one way, a finding that
moves the plan): tell them in Chat, the options a line each and yours, before you build on one.

A decision for the person: `colony gate "the question" --item R4 --why "what depends on it"`; where it has clear answers, `--option "..."` for each and
`--recommended "..."` for yours: they answer with a tap. The answer arrives as a note. A question whose moment is
later: `--when R12` keeps it off their list until R12 starts. Settled in conversation: `colony gate --answered ID
"their words" --context "your reading"`; no longer applying (superseded, moot): `colony gate --withdraw ID "why"`.
An open gate stays on their list. Where the world sets a time (a filing date, a shop's closing), `--by
2026-11-13T16:00` says so: it leads their Inbox from the day before (`--rise 7d` for longer), and nothing happens by
itself when it passes. One that can't be acted on before a day: `--from 2026-11-20` keeps it off their list until then.

When an answer first shows how they want a kind of decision made ("your call", or overruling one), ask once how it
should be decided from then on (ask each time, your judgement, or your judgement by their rule) and keep their
answer, in their words, with `colony rule`.
