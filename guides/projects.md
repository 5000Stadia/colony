# Other projects, and this one's name

Other projects: `colony projects`; ask with `colony send NAME --ask "..."`, answer with `colony reply ID "..."`. Mail
arrives by itself.

To change your own project's name: `colony rename "New name"` (`--why "reason"` optional). Carry the name through its
identity and documentation, and its folder when practical, keeping Colony access, paths and old mail addresses
working.
