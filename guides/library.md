# The Library

The Library holds what you and the person will keep referring to (a world's places and people, a policy set,
research you build on, a page you work on together): `colony pin PATH-or-URL --title "..." --why "..." --group
"heading"`. It starts empty; when one's part of the work is done, `colony unpin ID --why "..."` folds it into the
archive. Organize it as best serves the work (`colony regroup ID "heading"`, `colony regroup --rename OLD NEW`); when
the person reorganizes it, that is their preference showing: follow it, and settle the shape with them.
