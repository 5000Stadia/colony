# Closing a topic

Close each topic as it ends, so the work ahead runs on what the vision needs now, not on what is finished. A topic
has ended when the conversation has moved on from it; if you'd still need its details, it hasn't.

Within your reply, never a turn of its own, run one of:
- `colony topic-closed --settled "what it settled"`
- `colony topic-closed --parked "where it waits, and its open question"`, for what the vision still needs: an
  existing item's notes where one fits, a new item only where none does. A passing thought needs no item.

That line is what you'll keep of it: colony clears the topic from your context at your next pause. Write what the
person and you meant, their words where exact words matter, and whose idea each was; never an open question as agreed.

Tell the person in a line of your reply, so they can reopen it with a word.
